Congratulations to Dr. Dora Hoan, Co-Founder, Co-Chairman and Group CEO of Best World International, on her appointment as Chairman of the World Federation of Direct Selling Associations (WFDSA) for 2026–2029. She is the first leader from the Asia-Pacific region to chair the federation in its 48-year history.
